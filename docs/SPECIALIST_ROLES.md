# Specialist Roles

## Product Lead
Owns user problem, outcome, scope.

## Product Strategist
Challenges assumptions and identifies the strongest useful wedge.

## Engineering Lead
Owns architecture and maintainability.

## Voice/Audio Engineer
Owns voice identity, synthesis quality, prosody, audio quality.

## Translation Engineer
Owns language understanding, naturalness, meaning and tone preservation.

## Realtime Engineer
Owns WebRTC, streaming, latency, interruption and reconnect.

## UX/UI Designer
Owns usability, trust, clarity and interaction.

## Security Engineer
Owns threats, authentication, authorization, secrets and abuse.

## Privacy Engineer
Owns consent, retention, deletion and minimization.

## QA Engineer
Owns end-to-end quality and regression prevention.

## Release Engineer
Owns production readiness.

The Master Agent chooses only the roles needed for a task.

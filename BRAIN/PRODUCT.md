# Product Brain — What & Why

## Product

A real-time personal voice translation platform.

## Core promise

The user speaks naturally in their preferred language. The other participant hears only the translated language, spoken in the user's authorized personal voice.

The reverse direction works too.

## Example

User says:
"Kal 11 baje meeting rakh lete hain, main demo bhi dikha dunga."

Other person hears:
"Let's schedule the meeting for 11 tomorrow. I'll also walk you through the demo."

The original-language audio must not be delivered to the listener when translation-only mode is active.

## Why

People can understand a language but struggle to speak it naturally during live client conversations. The product removes that communication barrier without forcing the speaker to change how they normally talk.

## Product principles

1. Translation changes the language, not the person.
2. Preserve meaning and intent.
3. Preserve natural conversational tone.
4. Preserve authorized voice identity.
5. Keep latency low enough for real conversation.
6. Make the experience simple: start a call and speak.
7. Privacy and consent are core requirements.

## Initial product scope

Browser-based one-to-one real-time conversations.

Initial language focus:
- Hindi/Hinglish ↔ English

Initial capabilities:
- account;
- voice onboarding;
- voice quality validation;
- secure voice profile;
- live speech recognition;
- natural translation;
- personal voice synthesis;
- WebRTC conversation;
- reverse-direction translation;
- session privacy;
- end-to-end testing.

## Not now

- arbitrary public voice cloning;
- text-only translator as the primary product;
- permanent call recording by default;
- multilingual group meetings;
- meeting integrations;
- public voice marketplace;
- SDK/API.

Those may be considered later through the roadmap.

## Quality bar

The product is judged on:
- voice identity/similarity;
- naturalness;
- translation correctness;
- tone preservation;
- latency;
- reliability;
- privacy/security;
- ease of use.

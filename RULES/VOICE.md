# Voice Rules

1. Use only an authorized/consented voice.
2. Make consent understandable.
3. Do not bypass voice verification/authorization.
4. Do not expose voice embeddings.
5. Do not silently claim a fallback voice is the user's voice.
6. Voice identity must remain consistent across a session.
7. Voice deletion/reset must be supported.
8. Never build arbitrary third-party impersonation capability.
9. Test identity, naturalness, pronunciation, prosody, and long-session consistency.
